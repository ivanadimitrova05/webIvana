10 things you must see in Paris

  1. Eiffel Tower: The global symbol of Paris, built for the 1889 World's Fair, offering panoramic city views from its observation decks.
  2. Louvre Museum: The world's largest art museum housed in a former royal palace, home to the Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, and thousands of classical and historical treasures.
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral of Paris: A masterwork of French Gothic architecture featuring flying buttresses, intricate rose windows, and historic gargoyles.
  4. Basilique du Sacré-Cœur de Montmartre: A striking white-domed Romano-Byzantine basilica perched atop the highest point in the city with sweeping views.
  5. Musée d'Orsay: Located inside a grand Beaux-Arts railway station, it houses the world's finest collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist masterpieces by Monet, Van Gogh, and Degas.
  6. Arc de Triomphe: A massive neoclassical triumphal arch honoring those who fought for France, standing at the western end of the Champs-Élysées.
  7. Sainte-Chapelle: A royal medieval Gothic chapel renowned for its soaring 13th-century stained-glass windows depicting biblical scenes in vivid blues and reds.
  8. Tuileries Garden: Paris's oldest and most central public garden, stretching between the Louvre and Place de la Concorde with formal French landscaping and outdoor sculptures.
  9. Palais Garnier: A 19th-century opera house with a lavish Baroque interior, grand marble staircases, and a ceiling painted by Marc Chagall.
  10. Montmartre: The historic hilltop bohemian quarter known for its steep winding streets, local painters at Place du Tertre, and old-world village charm.

10 things you must see in London

  1. The Tower of London: A historic castle on the River Thames that houses the dazzling Crown Jewels and features the famous Beefeater guards. You can book entry tickets via the Historic Royal Palaces Website.
  2. Big Ben: The iconic Elizabeth Tower and the historic Gothic-revival building where the UK Parliament meets, situated right along the River Thames.
  3. The British Museum: A world-famous museum showcasing vast global antiquities, including the Rosetta Stone and ancient Egyptian mummies. Entry to the main collection is free.
  4. Buckingham Palace: The official London residence of the British monarch, where you can watch the famous Changing of the Guard ceremony.
  5. Tower Bridge: A 125-year-old combined bascule and suspension bridge featuring high-level glass walkways with panoramic river views.
  6. St. Paul's Cathedral: An architectural masterpiece designed by Sir Christopher Wren, featuring a massive dome that dominates the city skyline.
  7. Borough Market: A vibrant historic food market near London Bridge known for artisanal street food, fresh produce, and gourmet treats.
  8. The London Eye: A giant observation wheel on the South Bank offering 360-degree views of the city skyline and the Thames River.
  9. Covent Garden: A lively district filled with street performers, boutique shopping, craft markets, and historic theatre venues.
  10. Sky Garden: London's highest public garden, perched at the top of 20 Fenchurch Street, offering free 360-degree views of the financial district (reservations required in advance via the Sky Garden Website).

10 things you must see in Positano

  1. Church of Santa Maria Assunta: Located right in the heart of town, this church features a vibrant, majolica-tiled yellow and green dome and houses a 13th-century Byzantine icon of the Black Madonna. Entry is free.
  2. Roman Archeological Museum (Positano MAR): Situated 11 meters below the church, this subterranean museum lets you walk on glass platforms above a 2,000-year-old Roman villa preserved by the eruption of Vesuvius, featuring vivid Fourth Style frescoes.
  3. Spiaggia di Positano Marina Grande: The bustling main pebble beach right beneath the colorful cliffs. It is ideal for people-watching, swimming, or catching a boat, with both free public areas and private beach clubs like La Scogliera Positano.
  4. Fornillo Beach: A quieter, more relaxed alternative to the main beach. Reached via a scenic cliffside coastal walking path, it offers a more laid-back local atmosphere.
  5. Path of the Gods (Sentiero degli Dei): A dramatic high-altitude hiking trail running from Bomerano down toward Nocelle and Positano. It delivers sweeping panoramic views of the entire coastline and Capri.
  6. Via dei Mulini: Positano's main boutique-lined pedestrian street. It is packed with high-end designer clothing, handmade leather sandals, local art galleries, and ceramic shops.
  7. Franco's Bar: Perched next to the iconic Le Sirenuse hotel, this open-air terrace is the premier spot for sunset cocktails, craft drinks, and sweeping views over the illuminated vertical town.
  8. Arienzo Beach Club: Nestled in a private sunny cove known for getting the most daylight on the coast. You can reach it via a short boat shuttle from the main pier or by descending a long flight of stone steps.
  9. Music on the Rocks: A unique, vibrant nightclub carved directly out of a natural seaside cave right on the water.
  10. Li Galli: A tiny, myth-shrouded archipelago of small islands just offshore from Positano, best viewed or visited via a private boat tour along the coast.

10 things you must see in Santorini

  1. Oia Village & Sunset Viewpoint: Walk the marble-paved alleys, see the famous blue-domed churches, and watch the sun dip into the Aegean Sea from the Oia Castle ruins.
  2. Akrotiri Archaeological Site: Explore the remarkably preserved Minoan Bronze Age settlement buried beneath volcanic ash around 1600 BC.
  3. Fira to Oia Caldera Hike: Trek the 10-kilometer cliffside trail connecting the capital of Fira to Oia, passing through Firostefani and Imerovigli with panoramic views.
  4. Imerovigli & Skaros Rock: Visit the highest point of the caldera rim known as the "Balcony of Santorini," and hike out to the historic Skaros Rock promontory.
  5. Nea Kameni Volcano & Hot Springs: Take a short boat trip to the active volcanic islet of Nea Kameni to walk the crater rim and soak in nearby sulfur-rich thermal waters.
  6. Ammoudi Bay: Descend the stone steps below Oia to this small fishing port for fresh seafood or to try cliff-jumping near the tiny Saint Nicholas chapel.
  7. Red Beach & Black Sand Beaches: View the dramatic red lava cliffs at Red Beach near Akrotiri, or relax on the organized black volcanic sand and pebbles of Kamari Beach and Perissa Beach.
  8. Pyrgos Kallistis: Wander through this quiet, traditional medieval hilltop village featuring castle ruins, whitewashed lanes, and fewer crowds.
  9. Local Wineries & Wine Tasting: Tour volcanic vineyards and taste distinct Assyrtiko wines at historic estates like Santo Wines or Domaine Sigalas.
  10. Santorini Catamaran Cruise: Sail around the caldera waters, swim in the open sea, and look up at the towering white villages from below.

10 things you must see in Bled

  1. Lake Bled: The iconic emerald-green glacial lake surrounded by forests and the peaks of the Julian Alps.
  2. Bled Island (Blejski otok): A tiny island in the center of the lake that you can reach by a traditional wooden flat-bottom boat known as a Pletna.
  3. Pilgrimage Church of the Assumption of Mary: Located on Bled Island, featuring a 99-step staircase and a historic "wishing bell" you can ring inside.
  4. Bled Castle (Blejski grad): Slovenia's oldest castle, perched on a steep cliff high above the water, housing a museum, a chapel, and a printing press.
  5. Ojstrica Viewpoint: A short, steep 20-to-30-minute hike that delivers one of the most famous postcard panoramas of the lake, island, and castle.
  6. Mala Osojnica: A slightly more demanding 30-to-40-minute hike offering an even higher and broader perspective over the entire lake basin.
  7. Vintgar Gorge: A dramatic canyon just a few kilometers outside town where wooden walkways cling to vertical rock walls above the rushing Radovna River, leading to the Šum Waterfall.
  8. The Lakeside Promenade & 6 km Walking Loop: A flat, scenic path that takes about 1.5 to 2 hours to walk or bike all the way around the water.
  9. Straža Hill Summer Toboggan: A fun outdoor adventure spot above the lake where you can ride the alpine coaster or hike for alternate views.
  10. Lake Bohinj and Triglav National Park: A larger, quieter glacial lake and alpine wilderness located a short drive or day trip away in the heart of the Julian Alps.

10 things you must see in Budapest

  1. Fisherman's Bastion: Walk along the neo-Gothic and neo-Romanesque terraces on Castle Hill for fairytale turrets and sweeping, panoramic views across the Danube River to the Parliament Building.
  2. Hungarian Parlaiment Building: Marvel at the sprawling, neo-Gothic landmark on the Pest side of the river, which serves as the seat of the National Assembly and houses the Hungarian Crown Jewels.
  3. Széchenyi Thermal Baths: Soak in Europe's largest medicinal bath complex, featuring grand yellow neo-baroque outdoor and indoor pools heated by natural hot springs in City Park.
  4. St. Stephen's Basilica: Admire the towering neoclassical dome of the city's largest church and climb or take the elevator to the 360-degree observation deck for a full view of the capital.
  5. Széchenyi Chain Bridge: Stroll across Budapest’s most iconic 19th-century suspension bridge, which dramatically links the historic hills of Buda with the flat boulevards of Pest.
  6. Shoes on the Danube Bank: Reflect at this touching and solemn memorial made of iron shoes cast along the river edge, honoring the Jewish victims executed during World War II.
  7. Buda Castle ande Matthias Church: Ride the historic funicular up Castle Hill to explore the former royal palace complex and the striking, colorfully tiled 13th-century Matthias Church.
  8. Great Market Hall: Explore the bustling three-level 19th-century indoor market to sample local paprika, traditional sausages, fresh produce, and handmade Hungarian crafts.
  9. Ruin Bars (Szimpla Kert): Experience Budapest’s unique nightlife by stepping into Szimpla Kert, the pioneer of ruin pubs set inside eclectic, art-filled abandoned buildings in the Jewish Quarter.
  10. Gellért Hill: Hike up to the top of Gellért Hill for the best high-altitude sunset views of the bridges and the city skyline, alongside the Liberty Statue.
